Built for the part of your commute that car and train can't cover.

Most people live a few kilometres from the station and a few more from the office. Hinge covers both ends and then disappears.

  • 12 sec

    Folds without tools

    One latch at the hinge, one at the stem. It locks shut and rolls on its own wheels, so you can pull it like a suitcase.

  • 14 kg

    Light enough to carry up stairs

    An aluminium frame and a battery that sits inside the frame keep the weight low. Folded, it is 64 cm long and fits under most desks.

  • 60 km

    A week of commuting per charge

    The removable battery charges from a normal wall socket in about three hours, at your desk or at home.

  • 25 km/h

    Quick, but still a bicycle

    Pedal-assist only. The motor helps when you pedal and stops when you stop, so there is nothing to register or insure in most cities.
